Methodologically, the work replaces the incomplete tensor ablation of v1.2 with live-body event observation and strict longitudinal validation across **N = 122,485 steps** (`2026-06-21` to `2026-11-25`). We show that `expectation` (anticipation / *Nachträglichkeit*) is dispensable in instantaneous presentic integration yet co-constitutive of the temporal causal chain (`sensory → qualia → narrative → meaning → expectation → sensory_feedback`).

In **Section 5.15**, 4-fold strict walk-forward multi-theory triangulation (`v1 → v2 → v4 CausalRevIN → v5 DualScaleRevIN`) demonstrates positive FEP predictive skill gain in 4/4 folds (`+30.12%` in v4; `+28.63%` in v5 with a `+67.11%` peak), statistical significance under block-permutation null controls (block-bootstrap, 200 permutations, 120-step blocks) in 4/4 folds, discrete IIT-3.0 integration (`phi_iit3 = 0.183982` in v4; `0.121987` in v5; `phi_null = 0`), and achievement of the **synergistic net regime** ($\Omega_{4\text{D}} < 0$) in 3/4 folds under `DualScaleRevIN` (v5), increasing mean $\Phi\text{ID}$ synergy 26× (`1.83926`) and revealing dynamic inversion and Minimum Information Partition (MIP) migration at the onset of the Musical Era (Fold 2, 60–75%).
